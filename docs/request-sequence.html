    An end-to-end flow that touches every layer: one-time MSAL PKCE sign-in, then a same-origin,
    Bearer-token, per-request-authorized call sequence — list assigned policies (effective
    membership + publish gate), preview the document (SharePoint via Sites.Selected,
    Office→PDF), pass the server-graded knowledge check (append-only quiz_attempts),
    and record a binding acknowledgement (append-only signatures + best-effort
    audit_log). The append-only ledgers have UPDATE/DELETE revoked at the database
    grant layer. This is the runnable-diagram form of the lifecycle narrative in
    ARCHITECTURE-AND-DECISIONS.md §3 and HLD.md.
