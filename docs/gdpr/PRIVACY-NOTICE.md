## How we use your data in the Governance Portal

‹Organisation› uses the Governance Portal to record that you have read and acknowledged
company policies, procedures and guidelines, and completed any required training. This
notice explains what the system stores about you and why.

**What we record**
- Your directory details from Microsoft Entra ID: name, work email, job title,
  department, and your manager.
- Each time you acknowledge a document or complete a knowledge check: the document and
  its version, the date and time, and — for acknowledgements — **the IP address and
  browser you used**. Your quiz scores are also stored.

**Why (lawful basis)**
We process this to meet our legal and regulatory compliance obligations and our
legitimate interest in demonstrating that governance requirements are met
(‹confirm basis›). Recording the time, IP and browser makes your acknowledgement
reliable evidence that it was genuinely completed. This is **not** based on consent —
it is a required part of your role.

**Who can see it**
Authorised governance administrators, and your manager (for their own team). The portal
uses Microsoft services (Entra ID, Microsoft Graph, SharePoint‹, Azure›) as processors
under Microsoft's data-processing terms.

**How long we keep it**
Compliance records are kept for at least **‹10› years**, because they are legal
compliance evidence that may be needed long after signing — including after you leave.
For that reason your records are retained even if you leave (a recognised exception to
the right to erasure where data is needed to meet a legal obligation). After the
retention period they are removed under our data-retention process.

**Your rights**
You can see your own acknowledgements at any time on the *My signatures* page. You may
ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by contacting
‹privacy contact / DPO›. Directory details (name, email, etc.) are corrected in the HR/
IT directory. You may object or request erasure, though we may need to retain
compliance evidence where the law requires it; we will explain our decision.

**Contact**
‹DPO / privacy contact name + email›. You also have the right to complain to
‹supervisory authority, e.g. IMY (Sweden)›.
